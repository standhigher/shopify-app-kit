# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want this project to be useful, respectful, and practical for everyone who participates.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Be clear, kind, and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarification.
- Respect different levels of experience.

Unwelcome behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing, or repeatedly disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or other contributors.

If you need to report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers through the repository owner profile.
